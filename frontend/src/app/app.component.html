Who we are

One association.
Thousands of possibilities.

Business Correspondent Association Rajasthan is a registered trade union created to organize and represent Bank Mitras and Business Correspondents working at the last mile of financial inclusion.

From remote villages to growing cities, our members help citizens access banking. BCAR exists to ensure that this essential work receives the security, respect and opportunity it deserves.
