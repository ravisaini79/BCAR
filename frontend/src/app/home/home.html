Who We Are

About BCAR

Business Correspondent Association Rajasthan (BCAR) is a registered trade union formed to represent, support, and elevate the community of Bank Mitras and Business Correspondents (CSPs) across the state.

Our members operate in both urban neighborhoods and remote villages, providing essential banking services on behalf of leading commercial banks. We advocate for their rights, commission security, and welfare to ensure they work with dignity and progress.

WHAT DRIVES US

Our Pillars of Progress

A structured strategy to safeguard livelihoods and foster professional growth for every BC operator.

01

Fair Representation

We present members' grievances and commission structure concerns before commercial banks, corporate BCs, and regulatory bodies.

02

Social Security

We advocate for basic social security, including group medical insurance, pension benefits, and safety covers for remote field operations.

03

Professional Skills

We organize regular seminars, financial literacy campaigns, and training modules to update members on new banking tools and security protocols.

04

Statewide Network

We establish district-level subcommittees to handle local issues and ensure immediate support is available for members anywhere in Rajasthan.

Dear Business Correspondents & Bank Mitras,

"Business Correspondents are the backbone of the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) and the financial inclusion movement in Rajasthan. Yet, the challenges we face on the ground—ranging from inadequate commissions to the lack of social security—need urgent representation.

BCAR is committed to being your voice. We actively interface with banks, government departments, and corporate BC agencies to negotiate better working conditions. I urge every Bank Mitra in Rajasthan to join our union and strengthen our collective stand."
